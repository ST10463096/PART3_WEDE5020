## Website proposal

Websites are crucial for business to connect with customers, promote products, build credibility and services. They allow companies to share detailed information about their and enhance accessibility for users. This project aims to create a website for Nass Tech Solutions, a technology consulting firm, providing informative, user friendly, attractive and effective in increasing the company’s online presence and client base.

# 	Organization overview.

                                     
Established in 2019, Nass Tech Solutions is a consulting company focused on technology. The company provides IT services to small and medium-sized businesses needing help with digital systems and online services. The group was established to assist companies in conquering the difficulties involved with expensive and complex technological choices.

Nass Tech solutions is dedicated to delivering simple, affordable and effective solutions customized to meet the specific needs of every client. The business wants to assist businesses in raising their general performance, efficiency and productivity by means of technology.

## Mission.

                                       

Nass Tach Solutions aims to provide modern, trustworthy and reasonably priced technological solutions that advance operational effectiveness and foster corporate growth.

## Vision.

                                       
The company aims to become a trusted technology partner for small and medium-sized businesses by providing high-quality digital solutions and building strong relationships with its customers.

	Testing and quality Assurance

                                                     
Testing and quality assurance are vital while creating a website to guarantee it runs smoothly and offers a positive user experience. Before submission, the Nass Tech method will go through much testing.

Navigation testing will look at every link between web pages to make sure they work the way they should, such as being able to go back to the homepage and making sure there are no links that don’t work. To guarantee the website shows appropriately on many browser compatibility testing will encompass Google chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Mozilla Firefox.

Media testing will confirm images load properly and are well-prepared, while content testing will check grammar, spelling and style. The volunteer’s application form will be checked for correct data entry and functionality. Finally, HTML code will be checked to make sure it satisfies industry requirements.
